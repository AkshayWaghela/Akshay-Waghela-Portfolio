Credit Risk Scoring & Probability of Default Prediction

An end-to-end credit-risk workflow using historical application data, engineered features, chronological validation, XGBoost, sigmoid calibration, risk bands, and SHAP explainability.

PythonPySparkXGBoostscikit-learnSHAPStreamlit
PROJECT SNAPSHOTROC-AUC 0.706

05 / How I work

From raw data
to a defensible result.

A repeatable workflow helps make analysis easier to review, reproduce, and improve.

STEP 01

Frame the problem

Define the decision, target variable, constraints, and success criteria.

STEP 02

Prepare the data

Audit data quality, explore distributions, engineer features, and check leakage.

STEP 03

Validate the analysis

Choose suitable splits and metrics; assess assumptions, calibration, and robustness.

STEP 04

Explain & deliver

Communicate limitations, interpret results, and package useful outputs.
